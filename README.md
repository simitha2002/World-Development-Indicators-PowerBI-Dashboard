# 🌍 World Development Indicators (WDI) – Interactive Power BI Dashboard

This project was developed as part of a **Power BI Dashboard Competition**, where we designed a **multi-page interactive dashboard** using the **World Development Indicators (WDI)** dataset to analyze global development trends across key sectors.

The dashboard delivers a **complete sector-wise story of the world** — from **Population and Healthcare to Education, Electricity, Finance, and Trade**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To transform complex global development data into **meaningful, interactive, and decision-driven insights** using Power BI.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI  
- Power Query  
- Microsoft Excel    
- Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- Data Visualization & Storytelling  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Modules Overview

### 1. Population Analysis
- Total Population & Growth
- Male vs Female Population
- Urban vs Rural Population
- Population Trends Over Time

### 2. Healthcare Analysis
- Deaths due to Road Injuries & Chronic Diseases
- Suicide Death % Trends
- Health Expenditure Over Time
- Gender-wise Healthcare Analysis

### 3. Education & Unemployment
- Male vs Female Unemployment Rate
- Youth Unemployment
- Education Level vs Unemployment
- Top 15 Countries by Education Expenditure
- Unemployment Trends by Year

### 4. Electricity & Fresh Water
- Fresh Water Usage Over Time
- Top 10 Countries by Fresh Water Availability
- Electricity Usage by Sector (Agriculture, Industry, Domestic)
- Top Electricity Consuming Countries

### 5. Finance
- Central Government Debt
- Commercial Banks
- Interest Rates
- Top Tax Revenue Countries
- Top Countries by Government Debt

### 6. Trade
- Import vs Export Trends
- Fuel Imports vs Exports
- Trade Type Analysis
- Top Trading Countries

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Global population shows **consistent upward growth across all regions**
- **Urban population expansion outpaces rural growth**
- **Health expenditure peaks align with reduced suicide trends**
- **Higher education investment correlates with lower unemployment**
- **Government debt is heavily concentrated among a few major economies**
- **Fuel-driven economies dominate global trade exports**
